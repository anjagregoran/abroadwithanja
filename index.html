My storyMoja priča

Hi! My name is Anja and I come from Croatia.Bok! Ja sam Anja i dolazim iz Hrvatske.

In 2023, I spent a year on exchange in the U.S., with no idea just how much it would change my life. I went thinking I was leaving for just one school year, and that one year changed everything for me.Godine 2023. provela sam godinu dana na exchangeu u SAD-u, a nisam ni slutila koliko će mi to promijeniti život. Otišla sam misleći da idem samo na jednu školsku godinu, a ta mi je jedna godina promijenila sve.

That's also where I met my boyfriend. Now I spend a few months of the year with him in the USA, and in the summer he comes to visit me in Croatia, so today my life is somewhere between Croatia and the USA.Tamo sam upoznala i svog dečka. Danas nekoliko mjeseci godišnje provodim kod njega u SAD-u, a on ljeti dolazi k meni u Hrvatsku, pa je moj život danas negdje između Hrvatske i SAD-a.

Now, I share the experiences, advice, and things I wish someone had told me before I left Here you'll find exchange tips, stories and inspiration, and I teach you everything from how to apply to how to make it the best year of your life!Sada dijelim iskustva, savjete i sve ono što bih voljela da mi je netko rekao prije odlaska Ovdje ćeš naći savjete, priče i inspiraciju za razmjenu, a učim te svemu, od toga kako se prijaviti do toga kako da ti to bude najbolja godina života!
