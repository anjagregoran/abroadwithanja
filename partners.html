Who is behind this

I am Anja Gregoran. I spent the 2023/24 school year as a J-1 exchange student at a high school in Marcellus, Michigan, placed through International Student Exchange.

When I came home, students across the region started messaging me about how to apply and what it costs. I have been answering ever since, through free guides and my channel @abroadlifewithanja.

What we bring

  • Over 16,000 followers, mostly teenagers in the region who want a year in the USA
  • Recruitment across Croatia, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and North Macedonia, from one point of contact
  • English coursework summaries produced in house, since the company is a registered translation agency
  • Screening, documents and pre departure support, plus contact with parents throughout
