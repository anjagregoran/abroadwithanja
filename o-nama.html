Abroad Club USAAbroad Club USA

Pomažemo učenicima iz Hrvatske, Srbije i Bosne i Hercegovine da provedu godinu u američkoj srednjoj školi. Iza brenda stoji obiteljska tvrtka s uredima u Zagrebu i Rijeci.We help students from Croatia, Serbia and Bosnia and Herzegovina spend a year in an American high school. Behind the brand is a family company with offices in Zagreb and Rijeka.

Tvrtka iza brendaThe company behind the brand

Abroad Club USA je brend tvrtke OMNIA d.o.o., registrirane u Hrvatskoj od 2016. pri Trgovačkom sudu u Rijeci. Osnovna djelatnost tvrtke je profesionalno prevoditeljstvo, uključujući ovjerene prijevode sudskih tumača, u više od 25 jezika.Abroad Club USA is a brand of OMNIA d.o.o., a Croatian company registered since 2016 with the Commercial Court in Rijeka. Its core business is professional translation, including certified translations by sworn court interpreters, in more than 25 languages.

Tvrtku vodi Diana Jenul. Anja vodi dio za razmjenu i osoba je s kojom razgovaraš. U timu su još dvije kolegice, od kojih je jedna magistra engleskog jezika.The company is run by Diana Jenul. Anja leads the exchange side and is the person you will be talking to. Two more colleagues are on the team, one of them a master of English.

Zašto miWhy us

01

Prošli smo to samiWe have been through it

Godina u američkoj srednjoj školi, od prijave do povratka. Znamo kako se piše pismo obitelji domaćinu i kako se snima video za prijavu. Anja i danas provodi tri do pet mjeseci godišnje u SAD-u, ostala je bliska sa svojom obitelji domaćinom, a njezina host mama i sama radi u razmjeni.A full year in an American high school, from application to coming home. We know how the host family letter is written and how the application video is filmed. Anja still spends three to five months a year in the US, stayed close with her host family, and her host mom works in exchange herself.

02

Engleski vodi magistra engleskogEnglish led by a qualified teacher

Svaki program traži procjenu engleskog, a kod nas je ne radi netko usput. Vodi je kolegica s magisterijem engleskog jezika, koja drži i pripreme prije testa. Nitko ne izlazi na test nespreman.Every program requires an English evaluation, and ours is not done casually. It is run by a colleague with a master's degree in English, who also runs preparation before the test. Nobody takes the test unprepared.

03

Prijevodi se rade u kućiTranslations done in house

Prijepis ocjena, liječnička dokumentacija, preporuke. Sve mora biti prevedeno na engleski, a kod nas to rade naši sudski tumači. Ne šaljemo te nikamo i ne plaćaš to posebno.Transcripts, medical records, recommendation letters. All of it must be translated into English, and ours is done by our own sworn court interpreters. We do not send you anywhere else and you do not pay separately.

04

Put i aerodrom nisu problemThe travel part is easy

Ako nikad nisi letio sam, ovo djeluje kao najstrašniji dio, a zapravo je najlakši. Taj let do Amerike prošli smo više puta nego što možemo izbrojati. Proći ćemo s tobom i s roditeljima cijeli put.If you have never flown alone, this feels like the scariest part, and it is actually the easiest. We have taken that flight more times than we can count, and we walk you and your parents through the whole journey.

05

Prijava koja se primijetiAn application that gets noticed

Obitelji domaćini biraju učenika po pismu, fotografijama i videu. To je dio koji odlučuje, a najčešće se napravi na brzinu. Kod nas se radi polako i s nekim tko zna što te obitelji zapravo traže.Host families choose a student by the letter, the photos and the video. That is the part that decides everything, and it is usually rushed. With us it is done slowly, with someone who knows what families look for.

06

Iskreno o novcu i rokovimaHonest about money and deadlines

Reći ćemo ti ukupan trošak, ne samo naknadu programa. Avionska karta, viza, džeparac, školski troškovi. I reći ćemo ti ako nešto nije realno za tebe.We will tell you the total cost, not just the program fee. Flight, visa, pocket money, school costs. And we will tell you if something is not realistic for you.
